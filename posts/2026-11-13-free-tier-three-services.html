Three free tiers, tested: OpenRouter, Groq, Cloudflare

Bottom line first: all three are free, but each has its traps. OpenRouter is fastest to set up, Groq is fastest but easiest to get blocked, Cloudflare is the most stable. Keys are shown only once — save them in the vault right away, and never paste them in chat.

1. OpenRouter: fastest, ten minutes to set up

  • Free tier: models with :free in the name cost $0 per call; accounts that never bought credits get 50 free requests a day, max 20 per minute; refreshes daily at 00:00 UTC (08:00 SGT).
  • Sign up with Google login, create a key (starts with sk-or-v1-), and name it for the project.
  • Trap: a 429 means you hit the limit — wait for the 08:00 refresh, don’t delete the key and create a new one.

2. Groq: fastest, but don’t panic at 403

  • Free tier: rate limits vary by model and change over time — trust the Settings → Limits page in the console, not old numbers online.
  • Sign up with email, create a key (starts with gsk_); the API base is https://api.groq.com/openai/v1.
  • Trap: a 403 / error 1010 doesn’t mean the key is wrong — Cloudflare blocks the default User-Agent. Send a browser-style User-Agent and it works; don’t delete the key and create a new one. Whisper transcription covers about 8 hours of audio a day.

3. Cloudflare Workers AI: most stable, 10,000 Neurons a day

  • Free tier: 10,000 Neurons a day (official public number; after registering on 2026-11-13 the billing page didn’t show it directly — go by the Workers Free allowance), refreshes at 00:00 UTC (08:00 SGT); when it’s used up you get an error and it stops, no charge (error 3036).
  • Sign up, copy the Account ID (32 characters, not secret), and create a Workers AI token (shown once — store it in the vault).
  • Trap: some newer models need a paid plan — don’t pick them on the free tier; error 3036 means the day’s quota is used up, wait for tomorrow.

Two not worth trying

  • Cerebras: skipped. No permanent free tier — only a one-time $5 credit that expires 30 days after activation, and sign-up requires a payment method.
  • Cloady: registered (2026-11-13). $0.00/month; the long-term free workspace is real (0.5 vCPU / 1GB / 5GB, 10GB traffic a month, no card), but the free tier only runs in EU1, USE1, and USW1 — Singapore SIN1 was out of stock, so the workspace moved from the Japan default to Germany EU1. It pauses after 7 days without traffic and has no automatic backup, so it’s only good for light testing.

Figures verified against official public pages on 2026-11-13; quotas are as shown in the console after registration.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Who we are

Hokodaca builds AI tools and services for small and medium businesses. This policy covers our website at hokodaca.github.io, our digital products and services, and our internal publishing app, the Hokodaca YouTube Uploader. You can reach us at hokodaca@gmail.com.

Our website

This website is a static site. It has no user accounts, no sign-up forms and no comment system, and we do not run advertising or analytics trackers on it. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub may record standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under its own privacy policy.

The free Hokodaca Prompt Builder tool on this site runs entirely in your browser. Text you type into it is never sent to us or stored anywhere.

When you contact or buy from us

If you email us or buy a product or service, we receive the details you send us (for example your name, email address and what you ordered). We use them only to reply to you, deliver what you bought, and provide support. Payments are handled by the payment provider you check out with (for example PayPal or Gumroad); we never see or store your full card details.

  • We never sell your personal data.
  • We never share it with third parties except the providers needed to deliver your order or reply to you.
  • You can ask us at any time to delete the emails and order details we hold about you; write to hokodaca@gmail.com.

Hokodaca YouTube Uploader (Google API data)

The Hokodaca YouTube Uploader is an internal tool that Hokodaca uses to publish videos to Hokodaca's own YouTube channel. It signs in with Google OAuth and uses a single permission scope: youtube.upload (upload videos to our own channel).

  • The app accesses only Hokodaca's own YouTube account, authorized by Hokodaca itself. It is not offered to the public and no other person's Google or YouTube data is accessed, collected or stored.
  • The app does not read, store, share or sell any user data. Authorization tokens are kept in secure storage by the operator and are used only to upload our own videos.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's third-party access page (myaccount.google.com/permissions).
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and to the YouTube API Services Terms of Service.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. Material changes will be noted on our homepage.
